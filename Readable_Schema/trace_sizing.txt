MINIMUM PCB WIDTH FOR 25A CONTINUOUS CURRENT (IPC-2221)
===========================================================================

COPPER THICKNESS: 0.035 mm (1 oz)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Temp Rise (dT)    | External Layer Width    | Internal Layer Width
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
10 deg C          | 25.5 mm (1.00 in)       | 66.2 mm (2.61 in)
20 deg C          | 16.7 mm (0.66 in)       | 43.5 mm (1.71 in)
30 deg C          | 13.1 mm (0.52 in)       | 34.0 mm (1.34 in)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

COPPER THICKNESS: 0.070 mm (2 oz)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Temp Rise (dT)    | External Layer Width    | Internal Layer Width
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
10 deg C          | 12.7 mm (0.50 in)       | 33.1 mm (1.30 in)
20 deg C          | 8.4 mm  (0.33 in)       | 21.8 mm (0.86 in)
30 deg C          | 6.5 mm  (0.26 in)       | 17.0 mm (0.67 in)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

DESIGN NOTES FOR 170VDC / 25A MOTOR CONTROLLERS:
* MEASUREMENT: Widths refer to the narrowest "pinch point" in a fill zone.
* THERMAL RELIEFS: Disable thermal reliefs on high-current pads; use
  "Solid" connections to avoid fuse-like behavior at 25A.
* STITCHING: For high-power paths, mirror fills on Top and Bottom layers
  and connect with a dense via farm (1.0mm - 2.0mm grid).
* CLEARANCE: Maintain at least 1.25mm spacing between 170VDC fills and
  low-voltage logic to prevent arcing/creepage issues.
